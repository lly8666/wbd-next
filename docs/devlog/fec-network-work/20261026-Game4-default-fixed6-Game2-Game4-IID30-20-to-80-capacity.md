Source/parity is independently encrypted/PN/seq at actual lane send due. GameTx owns deep-copied typed source/parity payloads; no borrowed pointers back into FEC encoder.
- **Critical recovered bottleneck**: old `GameFecTx.poll` would await the **entire** GameTx drained before encoding the next parity 4-row quantum. Under a continuous 60ms future-copy schedule the queue need not become empty, so FEC was starved. New logic waits for *this quantum's actual native parity-copy credit*, or its bounded owner expiry. Once credited, original FEC job may progress/retire even though GameTx independently holds immutable future copies. Never overextend FEC encoder timeouts or receiver wait.
